Structure rules (what makes it read as DDG)
OmnibarTop pill: fully rounded (r=22), surface #F9F9F9 on white zone, 1px hairline border, shield-count left + orange fire right.
ButtonsFully rounded pills. Primary = blue #3969EF; destructive/fire = orange #DE5833; secondary = transparent with hairline.
CardsRadius 12–14, white (light) / #373737 (dark), hairline border, tiny shadow — no big elevation.
Favorites4-column grid, white letter tiles r=14, favicon letters tinted brand colors.
Tab switcherVertical list rows, not grid; raised orange fire button overlapping the bottom bar.
TypeDuck Sans (proprietary) → closest free match Inter. Titles 700, body 400–500, tight letter-spacing on headers.
