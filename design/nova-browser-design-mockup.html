Design exploration · v1

NOVA — a browser design that
belongs to none of them

Structure ideas borrowed from Chromium, soft-focus calm from Firefox, the instant-clear gesture from DuckDuckGo — then pushed through a new shape language (cut corners), a hexagonal action core, and a teal → violet → amber gradient none of the three use. This is the skin; the engine underneath is untouched.

The cut-corner design language

Every card in NOVA carries one chamfered corner — a small cut that no major browser uses. It becomes your signature: once you've seen it, any screenshot of the app reads as NOVA instantly.

Warm off-white paper replaces pure white, so the UI feels calmer in daylight mode while dark mode goes near-black blue.
